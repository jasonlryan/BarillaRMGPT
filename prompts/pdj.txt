        <Details/>
    </Slide>

    <Slide number="7">
        <Title/>
        <Details>
            <Subheadings_and_Descriptions>
                <Digital_Users>How people are connected to digital platforms and touchpoints.</Digital_Users>
                <Consumers>People's attitudes and behaviors when consuming pasta.</Consumers>
                <Shoppers>How people shop offline and online, and the shopper missions they pursue.</Shoppers>
                <Media_Audience>How people consume media and relevant touchpoints.</Media_Audience>
            </Subheadings_and_Descriptions>
            <Core_Definition_of_PDJ>
                <Item>Describes steps people take when deciding to buy or not buy categories or brands.</Item>
                <Item>The term 'People' is used to highlight their constant identity across planning, shopping, and consumption modes.</Item>
            </Core_Definition_of_PDJ>
        </Details>
    </Slide>

    <Slide number="8">
        <Title/>
        <Details>
            <Stages_of_the_Journey>
                <Awareness>Passive contact with the category or brand.</Awareness>
                <Consideration>Options (brands/products) are evaluated based on awareness, knowledge, or experience.</Consideration>
                <Purchase>People are ready to buy (omnichannel), seeking ease in the process.</Purchase>
                <Experience>Post-purchase, people use the product and connect with the brand.</Experience>
            </Stages_of_the_Journey>
            <Visual_Description>A flowchart-like structure visually represents the journey.</Visual_Description>
        </Details>
    </Slide>

        <Title/>
        <Details>
            <Steps>
                <Step_1:_Map_Behavior>
                    <Objective>Understand what is happening at each stage of the decision-making process.</Objective>
                    <Data_Sources>Integrated People Profile, dedicated PDJ study, and additional information.</Data_Sources>
                    <Key_Focus_Areas>
                        <Item>What people do.</Item>
                        <Item>Why, when, where, and how they do it.</Item>
                        <Item>Identifying barriers and touchpoints.</Item>
                    </Key_Focus_Areas>
                </Step_1:_Map_Behavior>
                <Step_2:_Define_Implications>
                    <Objective>Derive actionable steps based on behavior mapping to meet business objectives.</Objective>
                    <Focus_Areas>
                        <Item>Create demand for the category/brand.</Item>
                        <Item>Convert demand to the specific brand.</Item>
                    </Focus_Areas>
                    <Outputs>
                        <Item>Specific activities for targets.</Item>
                        <Item>Efficient allocation of resources (e.g., budget).</Item>
                    </Outputs>
                    <Diagram>
                        <Item>Box 1: Behavior Mapping (What do people do?)</Item>
                        <Item>Box 2: Implications (What we need to do?)</Item>
                    </Diagram>
                </Step_2:_Define_Implications>
            </Steps>
        </Details>

        <Title>Tips for Journey Mapping</Title>
        <Details>
            <Points>
                <Item>Map journeys per target (Bull's Eye and Footprint).</Item>
                <Item>Use a cross-functional team.</Item>
                <Item>Proceed step by step.</Item>
                <Item>Write in first person; avoid business jargon.</Item>
                <Item>Address knowledge gaps.</Item>
                <Item>Update the journey over time.</Item>
            </Points>
